# EPSG 25833 ETRS89 / UTM zone 33N

MySQL 8.4.11 exposes EPSG 25833 as `ETRS89 / UTM zone 33N`. It shares UTM
zone 33N's 15° central meridian, 0.9996 scale, and 500,000 m false easting,
but uses the GRS 1980 ellipsoid through ETRS89 rather than WGS 84. Fsdb now
uses the same Transverse Mercator implementation with those ellipsoid parameters.

For Oslo, MySQL transforms ETRS89 `POINT(59.9139 10.7522)` to
`POINT(262560.4821812616 6649443.583972801)`. Inverse transformation of
`POINT(262560.48218418856 6649443.584095765)` to WGS 84 yields
`POINT(59.91390000110276 10.752199999911273)`. The Expecto regression
checks these values; the differential contract also compares the full SRS
catalog row and rounded forward/reverse values.

This was the first projected ETRS89 system supported. The later
[ETRS89 UTM zone family](2026-10-11-etrs89-utm-zones.md) covers EPSG
25828–25837, while most of MySQL's SRS catalog remains open.
