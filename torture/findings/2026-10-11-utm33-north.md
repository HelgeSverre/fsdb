# EPSG 32633 WGS 84 / UTM zone 33N

MySQL 8.4.11 exposes EPSG 32633 as `WGS 84 / UTM zone 33N`, a metre-based,
easting/northing projected SRS. The catalog definition uses Transverse Mercator
with central meridian 15°, scale 0.9996, false easting 500,000 m, and no false
northing. Fsdb now supports its catalog row, planar metric operations, and
`ST_Transform` to and from the other supported WGS 84-based SRSs.

For Oslo, MySQL transforms geographic `POINT(59.9139 10.7522)` to
`POINT(262560.48218418856 6649443.584095765)`. The regression tests also
cover the equatorial zone edges (12°E and 18°E), 80°N, and inverse transforms.
The forward coordinates agree within 1 mm and inverse coordinates within
1e-8 degrees for those samples. The differential contract compares the full
catalog row, forward and reverse transform values, and planar distance.

The implementation uses the fourth-order Krüger Transverse Mercator series,
with the WGS 84 ellipsoid. This is an audited subset of MySQL's much broader
SRS catalog; behavior far outside UTM zone 33N remains unverified.
